# danielholzmann.com

Daniel Holzmann's portfolio: one page that is both the terminal-style site and the printed one-page CV.

## Language

**Skill**:
A field of work Daniel presents on the radar and in the list, such as AI agents or system integration.
_Avoid_: Focus area

**Skill level**:
How well Daniel masters a skill today, from 1 to 5. Set by hand; independent of experience.
_Avoid_: Focus level, proficiency

**Experience**:
Full years of hands-on work in a skill or career, counted from its start month to the build month, or to its end month for a skill no longer in use. Shown in years, capped at `10+`, on each skill's list row and bar.
_Avoid_: Seniority
