# danielholzmann.com

Daniel Holzmann's portfolio: one page that is both the terminal-style site and the printed one-page CV.

## Language

**Focus area**:
A field of work Daniel presents on the radar and in the list, such as AI agents or system integration.
_Avoid_: Skill

**Focus level**:
How much weight Daniel puts on a focus area today, from 1 to 5. Set by hand; independent of experience.
_Avoid_: Skill level, proficiency

**Experience**:
Full years of hands-on work in a focus area or career, counted from its start month to the build month.
_Avoid_: Seniority
